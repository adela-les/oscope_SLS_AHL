Digital Display:
2 Power Pin (3.3V); Pin 3v3
1 MOSI; PA7
1 CLK; PA1

Character Display:
11 GPIO Output Pin; PC2-12
3 Ground Pins; GND
1 Power Pin (5V); 5V

Inputs:
6 Input Pins for Encoders; E1:PB2 & PB3  E2:PB4 & PB5  E3:PB6 & PB7
1 Input Pin for Button; PC13

Single Channel Scope:
1 ADC Pin; PA0
