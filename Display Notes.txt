Display Notes

Character Display:
Expected would be voltage height "2.1 v Tall" example
Time width "20us Wide" example
You need a trigger level "1.0v"
You need rise or fall. 

Top row would be voltage left side and trigger right side
Bottom row would be time width left side and rise or fall right side. 

the Character display is two lines 16 characters each. 
Use Byte by Byte for Pinout
Alphabet is ASCII so will be 8 bits. You need to ensure you have correct order that's why we use byte for byte.

E is the clock
DI is RS that is where you would send data.


Wave Display:
It is 104 pixels wide by 64 pixels tall. The 1v would be in the middle of the width.
Need a few GPIO and SPI pins
